Voice commands

"theo" / "sora" / "hazel"Wakes that agent.
"keep listening"Open mic on: no wake word needed until it closes.
"stop listening"Open mic off; back to the wake word.
"full stop"Stops the reply and everything the agent started. Works without the wake word.
"new chat"Starts a fresh conversation.
"continue the chat about …"Reopens a saved chat.
"pass this to …"Hands the conversation to another agent.
"move this to the terminal"Continues the chat in a terminal session.
"goodbye"Ends the conversation.
